# M3-012 / M3-065 Generator-to-SONIC Evaluation

Date: 2026-08-22

## Protocol

- Motion: MRT2 M3 conditioned rollout, 1800 frames at 30 FPS.
- Audio: corresponding test-cache song, cropped from 4.267 s for 60 s.
- Execution: SONIC, 1.0x playback, full packet, 3 s measured-state alignment plus 1 s hold.
- Both runs survived the complete 60 s evaluation interval.
- Metrics are computed on the same 60 s clock. SONIC feedback is exported in the MuJoCo 29-DoF order.

SONIC retains 79.5% / 86.3% of the median joint amplitude for 012 / 065, but only
45.8% / 42.1% of total motion energy. This indicates substantial attenuation of fast
motion even when the robot remains upright. The reduced C4 boundary jump is partly
smoothing and must not be interpreted as generator continuity improving after execution.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BAS, music to motion | 0.243 | 0.350 | 0.283 | 0.268 |
| BAS, motion to music | 0.318 | 0.457 | 0.517 | 0.503 |
| Best onset-impact correlation | 0.028 | 0.032 | 0.014 | -0.005 |
| Best onset-impact lag (s) | -0.633 | -0.133 | -0.867 | 0.967 |

All onset-impact correlations are below 0.10 and therefore their fitted lags are not
reliable. BAS increasing after SONIC smoothing in sequence 012 demonstrates that BAS alone
cannot measure dance quality or prove that the generator causally uses music. Causal music
conditioning still requires paired/shifted/shuffled/silence generation under fixed noise.

## Interpretation limits

- These are two fixed trajectories and one SONIC repeat per trajectory, not population results.
- Automatic metrics do not establish aesthetic quality; blinded human evaluation is still needed.
- Foot-skating and ground-contact values are diagnostic FK proxies, not measured contact forces.
- Diversity, FID/FMD, and style coverage require a multi-song, multi-seed generated set and GT baseline.
